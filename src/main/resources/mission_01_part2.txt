SCENE 3 — THE KINGDOM GATES

Ophelia leads you through the last stretch of cobblestone road. Ahead,
two massive iron gates loom against the dark sky — the boundary between
the safety of Aeloria and whatever dangers lie beyond.

The guards posted at the gates nod to Ophelia as you approach. She
turns to face you, hovering at eye level, her expression serious for
the first time since you met her.

OPHELIA:
"This is as far as I go for now. Beyond these gates, you're on your
own — but I meant what I said back there. I'm never too far."

She pauses, then smiles softly.

OPHELIA:
"Be brave. The King chose well."

She gives a small wave, then drifts back toward the city.

You face the gates. The guards pull them open with a long, heavy groan of iron.

The road stretches out before you into darkness.

You take your first step beyond the walls of Aeloria—

A figure steps out from the shadows beside the road.

Tall. Cloaked. A jagged scar runs across his jaw. His hand rests on the
hilt of a blade at his side. He grins.

BANDIT:
"Well, well. Look at what the castle spat out."

He tilts his head, eyes scanning you up and down.

BANDIT:
"Word travels fast in these parts, hero. They say the King sent his
little champion off to fetch the Ember back."

He steps closer, hand tightening on his blade.

BANDIT:
"I've got a better idea. Hand over your gold and those pretty potions
of yours... and maybe I'll let you walk away with all your teeth."

He draws his sword.
