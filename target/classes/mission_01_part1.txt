MISSION ONE: THE ETERNAL EMBER

A darkness has fallen over the Kingdom of Aeloria.

For centuries, the Eternal Ember has burned at the heart of the kingdom —
a sacred flame gifted to the people by the Ancient Ones, a source of light,
warmth, and life itself. Crops grew beneath its glow. The sick were healed
by its warmth. The kingdom prospered under its radiance.

Until the night it was stolen.

The Dark Sorcerer Malachar descended upon the Ember's Sanctum without
warning, laying waste to the royal guards and vanishing into the cursed
eastern wastelands with the Eternal Ember in his clutches. Since that
terrible night, the skies above Aeloria have grown grey, the harvest has
withered, and fear has crept into every corner of the kingdom.

Now the King has called upon only one person — you.

SCENE 1 — THE THRONE ROOM

The great doors of the throne room swing open. You step inside, your
footsteps echoing across the cold stone floor. Towering pillars line the
hall on either side, their torches flickering as if the very flames are
afraid. At the far end, seated upon a throne of carved oak and silver, is
King Aldric. Beside him hovers a small, luminous figure — a fairy draped
in shimmering green light.

The King rises from his throne as you approach. His eyes are heavy with
worry, but when they land on you, something steadies in his gaze.

KING ALDRIC:
"Ah. You've come."

He descends the steps of the throne slowly, hands clasped behind his back.

KING ALDRIC:
"[PLAYER NAME], the Kingdom is in great danger and we need your help.
Malachar — a Dark Sorcerer of terrible power — has stolen the Eternal
Ember. Without it, our land grows cold and dark. Our people suffer.
And each day that passes, Malachar grows stronger."

He stops before you, meeting your eyes directly.

KING ALDRIC:
"I have looked to every corner of this kingdom for someone bold enough
to face him. And I keep finding myself looking at you. I see great
potential in you — a strength that runs deeper than a sword arm or a
quick spell. I truly believe it is YOU that will save this Kingdom."

The King places a firm hand on your shoulder.

KING ALDRIC:
"Can you help our Kingdom?"

KING ALDRIC:
"I knew you would not let us down."

He nods to the fairy beside him. She drifts forward, her wings catching
the torchlight in tiny rainbows.

KING ALDRIC:
"This is Ophelia. She is one of my most trusted fairy companions and she
will accompany you on your journey — at least to see you safely to the
kingdom gates. Listen to her well."

OPHELIA:
"Hello, brave one! I've heard so much about you."

She curtsies midair with a bright smile.

OPHELIA:
"Don't worry — I'll make sure you're well prepared before you step
beyond those gates. We'll stop by the Night Market on the way. The King
has arranged something for you there."

King Aldric steps back toward his throne and reaches into the folds of
his royal cloak. He produces a small leather pouch, heavy with the clink
of coins, and holds it out to you.

KING ALDRIC:
"Take this gold. Use it to purchase potions at the Night Market before
you depart. You will need them where you are going."

He presses the pouch firmly into your hand.

KING ALDRIC:
"Go now, brave one. The kingdom is counting on you. Return the Eternal
Ember — and may the light guide your path."

SCENE 2 — THE NIGHT MARKET

Ophelia leads you through winding castle corridors and out through a side
passage that opens into the city below. Even in the darkness, the Night
Market hums with lantern light and the murmur of late-night traders.
Stalls draped in coloured cloth line the cobblestone lane, and the smell
of herbs and strange powders drifts through the cool air.

Ophelia flutters to a stop in front of a stall lined with glowing bottles
in every shade — ruby red, deep violet, shimmering gold.

OPHELIA:
"Here we are! This is Old Mira's Potion Stall — the best in all of
Aeloria. Whatever you buy here, you can trust it."

She gestures to the rows of vials with a delicate hand.

OPHELIA:
"The King's gold should cover a few potions to get you started. I'd
recommend a Health Potion at the very least — the road ahead won't
be gentle."
